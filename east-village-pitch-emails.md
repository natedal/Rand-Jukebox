## Email Addresses to Research

You'll need to find the contact emails for these bars. Here are some ways to find them:

1. **Check their websites** - Most bars have contact forms or email addresses
2. **Social media** - Instagram/Facebook often have business email addresses in bio
3. **Google Business listings** - Often include contact emails
4. **Call directly** - Ask for the manager/owner's email

## Recommended Approach

1. **Send individually** - Personalize each email slightly based on any recent posts/events
2. **Follow up** - If no response in 5-7 days, send a brief follow-up
3. **Offer demo** - Suggest meeting in person to show them the app
4. **Be flexible** - Mention you're open to customization based on their needs

## Next Steps After Interest

1. **Schedule demo** - Show them the app in person
2. **Spotify setup** - Help them connect their Spotify Premium account
3. **Training** - Show them the admin dashboard
4. **Soft launch** - Test with staff first, then customers
5. **Feedback loop** - Iterate based on their experience

## Pricing Discussion (When Asked)

Consider these options:
- **Free trial period** - 30 days to test
- **Monthly subscription** - $50-150/month per venue
- **Revenue share** - Small percentage if they monetize it
- **Free for feedback** - In exchange for testimonials and referrals

Good luck! 🎵
